02 / Strategy

Stink bids and sweeps

The main strategy was to leave orders far from the reference price, catch large trades sweeping through the book, and try to exit on the bounce. Selected holdings were kept for settlement.

STRATEGY

Catch the sweep

Early orders sat 6–20 cents from external fair value, sometimes one tick ahead of competing walls. Later ladders sat 3/4/5 cents from SIG’s own price. Exits aimed to recycle cash after a bounce; when it never came, inventory stayed open.

Match the contracts

The bot filtered stale and wide quotes before comparing prices. The contracts also had to mean the same thing: we caught governor markets incorrectly mapped to lieutenant-governor markets.

03 / Tests

Tighter quotes weren’t enough

A market-making replay looked profitable when it put our orders first in line. Adding a more realistic queue reversed the result. That experiment stayed in shadow mode.

Cash mattered just as much

A large Rhode Island NO holding tied up capital. Trading an extra slice around it still needed cash for buybacks. Before election night, cash recovered to roughly 500,000, then nearly all of it went back into positions.

04 / Election night

Where it went wrong

The account entered the evening near 1.016 million, in fourth place. A bad automatic exit cost money first. Losses on the remaining holdings followed.

MN-01

An old exit setting survived a reversal

A manual purchase turned a small short into a large long. The bot kept the old exit price and sold 50,000 newly bought contracts, losing 25,939.53 against their purchase cost, before any unshown charges.

After the last trade

The positions kept losing value

Trading stopped around 00:47 EST. The holdings then stayed unchanged, but the account lost another 284,276.56 in leaderboard value by the post-close snapshot.

Stale exits

The old short-position exit remained active after a manual trade created a new long.

Repeated bets

Democratic YES and Republican NO expressed the same directional bet.

Missing prices

The closing reference feed omitted 17 holdings. Its incomplete total could not be used as portfolio value.
